# GSL-Votes

The Weber County Hive / Utah Hive Politics

Plain-language records of how Utah legislators voted on Great Salt Lake and water bills. Each page checks what a legislator said against the Legislature's own roll calls, and grades each bill on the Hive's collective rubric (Power, Transparency, Financial accountability, Environmental impact, Community impact).

First page: Rep. Jason Kyle (House District 8), every Great Salt Lake and water vote, 2023–2026. The data is in `kyle-gsl-votes-2023-2026.csv` and `kyle-gsl-votes-2023-2026.json`.
